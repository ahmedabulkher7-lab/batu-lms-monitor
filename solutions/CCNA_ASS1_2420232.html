Part 1: Design an IP Addressing Scheme (Step 1 Questions a – f) Subnetting Analysis
a. Based on the topology, how many subnets are needed?
5 Subnets (4 for the LANs + 1 for the WAN serial link between R1 and R2).
b. How many bits must be borrowed to support the subnets?
3 bits (since 22 = 4 < 5, and 23 = 8 ≥ 5 subnets).
c. How many subnets does this create?
23 = 8 total subnets (each with a block size of 32 addresses).
d. How many usable hosts does this create per subnet?
25 - 2 = 32 - 2 = 30 usable host addresses per subnet (≥ 25 required).
